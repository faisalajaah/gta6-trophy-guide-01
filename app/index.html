The offline trophy guide & tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 — every one of the 55 official trophies, in our own words, with sources.

55 trophies: 1 platinum · 2 gold · 6 silver · 46 bronze · 28 hidden · 1 possibly missable · all offline, one playthrough, about 200 hours.

0/ 55 earned

Sources. Trophy names, descriptions and grades: the official PS5 list on Exophase and PSNProfiles. Methods and the roadmap: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, checked against Push Square and KeenGamer. Every guide on this page is written in our own words from those sources; where a detail is still unconfirmed, the guide says so. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this app.

Reading the tags. Every trophy carries its roadmap stage (“Roadmap: story”, “alongside the story”, “strangers”, “cleanup”, “platinum”) and the 28 trophies PlayStation hides until earned are marked “Hidden on PSN”. The spoiler setting hides story trophies until you earn them; the tags never name a hidden trophy.

Offline. This guide runs entirely from its own files and reaches for no network. Your progress and notes live only in this browser (localStorage) until you export them.
