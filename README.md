Leonida Ledger — the offline trophy guide & tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. All 55 official trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) with their official names, descriptions and grades; a checklist with progress saved in the browser, export and import; filters by grade, kind, story, missable, collectibles and activities, plus search; a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until earned; a three-step roadmap to the platinum; the one possibly missable trophy flagged with its full warning; and a guide to every trophy in our own words, each citing its sources (Exophase and PSNProfiles for the official list, PowerPyx, Push Square and KeenGamer for the methods). No Rockstar logo or official artwork anywhere; the app runs entirely from its own files and reaches for no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
